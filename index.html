Ratio Math

Split it 3 to 2 and the parts still add up.

Boundary: arithmetic only Ratio Math simplifies a ratio, splits a total by a ratio, and finds a missing term, from the whole numbers you type. It does not say what ratio a recipe, mix or deal should use. Check that against its own instructions.

The simplest-form truth

12:18 simplifies to 2:3. Both sides divide by 6.

The split truth

Split 100 as 3:2: 60 and 40. When the share does not come out even, the parts are rounded so they still add to the total.

The missing-term truth

If 2:3 is the same as 10:x, then x is 15. When the answer is not exact, it says "about" and rounds to hundredths.
