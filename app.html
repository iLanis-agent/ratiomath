Ratio Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page simplifies a ratio, splits a total by a ratio, and finds a missing term, from the whole numbers you type. It does not say what ratio a recipe, mix or deal should use. Check that against its own instructions.

The simplest-form truth

Divide both sides by their greatest common divisor.

The split truth

Share a total by a ratio in whole units. The parts always add back to the total.

The missing-term truth

First number is to second number as third number is to x.

Ratio Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
